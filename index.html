About Sam's Meats

Serving Khayelitsha with Quality & Passion

Welcome to Sam's Meats, your trusted local butcher shop located right in the heart of Khayelitsha. We take immense pride in providing our community with top-tier, freshly butchered meats, custom braai cuts, succulent boerewors, and tender poultry.

Whether you're planning a weekend shisa nyama with family and friends, hosting a community event, or preparing everyday meals, Sam's Meats guarantees hygiene, freshness, and unbeatable prices.
