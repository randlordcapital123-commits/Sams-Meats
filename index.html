About Sheba Town

Student living that actually works for you

Sheba Town Student Accommodation is a modern, secure and fully managed residence in Gauteng, designed around the real needs of students. From the moment you move in, everything is set up so you can focus on your studies — reliable power, fast internet, clean shared spaces and a community that feels like family.

  • 24/7 Security & Access Control

    Biometric gate, CCTV and on-site house managers.

  • Backup Power & Water

    Load-shedding and outage ready — always connected.

  • Uncapped Fibre Wi-Fi

    Fast, stable internet in every room and study area.

  • Cleaning & Laundry

    Weekly cleaning of shared spaces and affordable laundry.

  • Campus Shuttle

    Scheduled transport to major campuses and malls.

  • Quiet Study Lounges

    Dedicated desks, group rooms and printing station.
